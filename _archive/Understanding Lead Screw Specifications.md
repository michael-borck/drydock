What does T8, 4 Lead, 2mm pitch mean in a trapezoidal lead screw 

***

**Assistant:**

In the context of a trapezoidal lead screw, the terms "T8," "4 lead," and "2mm pitch" refer to specific characteristics of the screw's thread profile.

1. 
T8: T8 is a metric designation that represents the thread size or profile of the screw. It indicates the nominal diameter of the screw's threaded portion. In this case, T8 implies that the screw has a nominal diameter of 8mm.

2. 
4 Lead: The term "lead" refers to the linear distance traveled by the screw for each complete revolution. In a trapezoidal lead screw, multiple starts or threads can be present on the screw's surface. Each start or thread constitutes a "lead." Therefore, "4 lead" means that there are four separate threads or starts on the screw.

3. 
2mm pitch: The pitch is the distance between corresponding points on adjacent threads. In this case, the pitch is specified as 2mm, meaning that the distance from one point on a thread to the corresponding point on the adjacent thread is 2mm.



To summarize, a trapezoidal lead screw described as "T8, 4 Lead, 2mm pitch" would have a nominal diameter of 8mm, four separate threads (leads), and a pitch of 2mm, indicating the distance between corresponding points on adjacent threads.
