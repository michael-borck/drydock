# Identifying E3D Nozzle Size

**Human:**
how can I tell the nozzle size on te E3D nozzle

***

**Assistant:**

To determine the nozzle size on an E3D nozzle, you can follow these steps:

1. 
Check the markings: Most E3D nozzles have size markings engraved or stamped on them. Look closely at the base of the nozzle, where it attaches to the heating block. The size is typically indicated by a number, such as 0.4, 0.6, or 0.8, representing the nozzle diameter in millimeters.

2. 
Use a caliper: If the markings are not clearly visible or absent, you can use a caliper to measure the nozzle diameter directly. Ensure that the nozzle is clean and free from debris. Open the caliper jaws and carefully place them around the nozzle, making sure they are aligned with the widest part of the nozzle opening. Gently close the caliper until it contacts the nozzle walls, and read the measurement on the caliper display.

3. 
Check the product documentation: If you are unsure about the nozzle size, refer to the product documentation provided by E3D. The documentation should specify the size of the nozzle you have or provide guidance on how to determine the size.

4. 
Consult the packaging or purchase records: If you still have the packaging or purchase records for your E3D nozzle, check if the nozzle size is mentioned there. The packaging or documentation provided with the nozzle may include the size information.



Remember that the nozzle size determines the diameter of the filament extruded from the 3D printer, which in turn affects the resolution and speed of the printing process. It's important to have an accurate measurement to ensure compatibility with your 3D printer and achieve the desired print quality.
