# Drydock: keep the fleet printing

**The Prusa feel: set and forget.**

A printer you own is a printer you can keep.

---

## The mission

1. **Give a printer the Prusa feel: set and forget.**
2. **It is not speed.** If you need to print faster, get another printer, as cheaply as you can.
3. **Don't retire a machine that still works** because something newer came along.

Our first MK3S felt like set and forget after an Ender. Everything here is an attempt to get that feeling back, or onto hardware that never had it.

---

## Restore, don't replace

That is the default, and it has limits. If a repair or restoration costs more than a new printer, buy new, unless you are sure of better quality or speed, you are trying to prove a point, or you just love the challenge. Have a reason to spend the money.

The comparison price is low. Amalgam, the sister project, aims at a whole printer for under $300 AUD.

---

## The claim

The MK2 and MK3 are still good machines. A maintained MK3S prints functional parts to the same quality as a new $300 printer. It does it slower. That is the whole trade, and it is a fair one.

What has moved on is not the quality ceiling. It is the quality floor, the quality per hour, and the amount of tuning a new machine hides behind auto-calibration. A neglected MK3 prints worse than an A1. A refreshed MK3 prints as well or better for the work most people actually do.

## The refusal

We reject the appliance model: a cloud account to slice, an app to monitor, a closed board that ends the machine's life when a part fails. A printer is a tool. A tool you cannot repair is a subscription.

## The rules

1. **Quality over speed.** We measure parts, not marketing numbers.
2. **Repairable by design.** Every part has a source and a substitute.
3. **Offline by default.** No account, no cloud, no kill switch.
4. **Documented, not remembered.** A procedure outlives the person who knew it.
5. **Honest about limits.** We say what an MK3 cannot do, so expectation does not become disappointment.
6. **Same lineage as Amalgam.** Build from scrap, or revive what exists. Both are sovereignty.
7. **Experiments ask one question.** How far can set and forget go? What is learned gets written up.

## What quality means here

Three things get called "print quality." We separate them.

- **Best case, slow and tuned.** Parity with modern printers. This is where the MK3 holds its ground.
- **Quality at 150-300 mm/s.** We are behind, by physics. Buy a CoreXY if this is the requirement.
- **Consistency with zero effort.** We are behind, and we close it with maintenance and tested profiles rather than hardware.

The full table is in [expectations.md](expectations.md). Publish it. Over-promising is what kills a community.

## Who this is for

Someone with a used or inherited Prusa. A school that cannot use cloud printers. A shop that needs a backup machine. A person who wants to fix their printer in 2036.

If you want set-and-forget at speed, buy an appliance. If you want a machine you own, read on.
